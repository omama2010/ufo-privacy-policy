UFO Heist (UFO大強奪) Privacy Policy

UFO Heist (UFO大強奪), provided and distributed by おまま開発室 (Omama Development Studio, referred to below as “the Studio”), uses external services to provide online rankings. This policy explains how user data is handled in this app.

1. External services

  • Google Firebase (provided by Google LLC): sending and receiving online ranking data and authentication.

2. Data collected and transmitted

The following information is transmitted and stored to provide online rankings:

  • Clear time for each stage, in milliseconds
  • A timestamp indicating when the record was achieved
  • An anonymous user identifier (a random ID issued by Firebase Anonymous Authentication)

This information does not identify a specific individual.

3. Information that directly identifies individuals

This app does not ask users to enter or collect information that directly identifies them, such as their name, address, phone number, email address, credit card information, or location. It also does not use behavioral advertising SDKs or collect tracking IDs.

4. Purposes of data use

Collected data is used only for the following purposes:

  • Providing and displaying online rankings
  • Matching personal best records and ranking positions
  • Preventing fraudulent score submissions and maintaining stable server operation

5. Disclosure to third parties

Except where required by law, collected data will not be sold or provided to third parties without the user's consent.

6. Data security

All transmitted data is protected using encrypted HTTPS/SSL communications. Appropriate access controls are applied through Firebase security rules.

7. Data retention and deletion

Unless deletion is requested, anonymous user identifiers (Firebase anonymous UIDs) and online ranking records (stage, clear time, and the time the record was achieved) are retained without a fixed expiration date.

To request deletion, contact tabasco_lv99@yahoo.co.jp and state that you are requesting deletion of UFO Heist data. After confirming the relevant data, we will promptly delete the anonymous user identifier and associated online ranking records.

8. Changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy may be revised when app features or applicable laws change. The latest version will always be published on this page.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy or how data is handled in this app, please use the developer contact information on the app's Google Play store listing.
